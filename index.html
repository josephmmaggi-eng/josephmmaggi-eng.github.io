Automated Robotic Grinding Cell

Senior design capstone — full lifecycle ownership from architecture to validated production capability.

RoleTeam Lead — tooling, controls & electrical
PlatformFANUC robot + stepper-driven fixture axis
Workpiece30″ stainless steel, surface finishing
StatusValidated · 2025

A production shop needed automated grinding to meet new contract demand, but the turnkey robotic cell they were quoted — just over $300,000 — was far beyond their means. My team's answer: a smaller FANUC robot paired with a custom stepper-driven moving fixture that translates the part through the robot's work envelope, delivering the process at a small fraction of the cost.

I owned the end-of-arm tooling, the fixturing, and the entire electrical build — then commissioned the cell through the failures every real start-up produces.

Validated Results
Cycle time15:00 → 9:30 per part (−40%)
Throughput~7 parts/hr vs ~4 manual (≈ +75%)
Capital costSmall fraction of the ~$300k turnkey quote
ConsistencyImproved surface finish; reduced regrind/repolish rework

SwitchWeld Cobot — Shipped Components

Robotic Technologies of Tennessee — component design for a commercial collaborative welding robot.

RoleProject Engineering Intern
PlatformSwitchWeld collaborative welding robot
Process3D-print prototype → CNC machinability review → release
StatusShipped in product

Three components I designed made it into the final commercial product. Each one cycled through printed prototypes and machinability reviews with the in-house manufacturing engineer before release — imagery is withheld out of respect for the company's IP, but the work is described below and I'm glad to discuss it in detail.

Welding torch end-effector mount Shipped

A precise, repeatable EOAT interface designed within the robot's fixed mounting-hole constraints — built to hold position under welding loads.

Quick-detach cable guide system Shipped

Quick-detach at both the arm mount and the cable itself, with clearance geometry that keeps the routing clear of the cobot's joints through its full range of motion.

Operator control box Shipped

Owned the electrical layout, wiring, and wiring-diagram creation — emergency stop, pause, and start circuits for the production system.

Arc-current insulation disc

Researched dielectric materials and designed a barrier to keep weld arc current from traveling up the arm and damaging the robot's internals.
